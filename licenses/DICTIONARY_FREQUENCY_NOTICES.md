# Dictionary frequency ranking

models/cael-dictionary-wn30/frequent.txt is the English 2018 50,000-word frequency
list from FrequencyWords, Hermit Dave and contributors, based on OpenSubtitles
2018: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/blob/master/content/2018/en/en_50k.txt
